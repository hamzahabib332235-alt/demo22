About Us

Global news reporter was founded in 2019 as a digital media platform dedicated to connecting global information hubs. Our team spans karachi, New York, London, Tokyo, and Dubai — delivering international-depth reporting through a local lens.

We believe quality information should never be blocked by language or geography. By integrating multilingual sources, leveraging geolocation technology, and employing data visualization, we make every news story spatially aware — you don't just know "what happened," you see "where it happened."

From breaking news to cultural events, from economic data to city walks — Global news reporter is your companion for understanding the world.
